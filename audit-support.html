02Choose your coverage

Two levels. Same team behind both.

The work product is identical. The difference is how long the coverage window stays open.

Basic
$495 one-time
3-year coverage

Covers an IRS examination of the tax year your study is first claimed, for any exam opened within three years of filing that return, the normal IRS assessment window, where the vast majority of examinations begin.

Most protection Extended
$1,995 one-time
10-year coverage

Covers your study's positions on any tax year it affects, for any exam opened within ten years. Because each year you claim your study's depreciation carries its own assessment window, your classifications stay defensible on a rolling basis, for the full life of the accelerated deductions.

An important distinction

Audit Support covers explaining and substantiating your study: the documentation and reasoning behind the numbers. It is not formal IRS representation. If you'd like a credentialed professional to correspond with or appear before the IRS on your behalf, that's available as a separate engagement through one of our licensed tax professionals qualified to represent you before the IRS. Just reach out and we'll connect you.

04The fine details

What's covered, and what isn't.

Audit Support covers the cost segregation study and its original figures, within the window of the tier you choose.

Covered

  • Explaining and substantiating your study's classifications and recovery periods
  • The original figures and allocations in your report
  • The methodology memo, workpapers, and consultation to prepare your response

Not covered

  • Formal IRS representation (available separately through one of our licensed tax professionals)
  • Examinations arising from fraud, willful evasion, or an unfiled return
  • Items on your return unrelated to the study; taxes, penalties, or interest
  • Any guarantee of a particular audit outcome
