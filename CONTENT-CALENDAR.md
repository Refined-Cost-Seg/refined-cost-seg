## Cadence notes

- Aim for ~700–1,000 words per post — long enough to rank, short enough to actually publish weekly.
- Always include: a target keyword in the title and first paragraph, one internal link (to another post or the homepage calculator/contact), and the standard "not tax advice" disclaimer.
- Vary the author between Eugene (tax/strategy angle) and Ethan (investor/market angle) for range.
- Keep the brand voice: plain-spoken, candid, never hype. "Audit-ready," never "audit-proof."
