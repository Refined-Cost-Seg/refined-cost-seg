Sample study package

What every client receives

Each study is delivered as three files: the report, a one-page summary for the tax preparer, and a fixed-asset schedule ready to import into depreciation software. These samples are for a lakefront short-term rental, and all three tie to the cent.

  • PDF
    Sample Cost Segregation Report

    Results, first-year impact, asset detail by recovery class, improvements, method and the ATG index.

  • PAGE
    Sample Preparer Implementation Summary

    The one-page guide for the tax preparer: basis by class, Form 4562 line mapping, convention and state notes. Prints to one page.

  • CSV
    Sample Fixed-Asset Schedule

    Every component with class, method, convention, in-service date, basis and bonus %, for direct import.

Sample figures are illustrative. Each study is prepared from the client's own documents and finalized after a quality-control review. Nothing here is tax advice; the preparer of record decides how a study is applied.
